5 Marla House Construction Cost in Pakistan (2026 Complete Material & Labor Breakdown)

Building a residential home in Pakistan requires thorough planning, understanding of current market rates for building materials, and reliable on-ground supervision. For middle-class and overseas Pakistani families, a 5 Marla house (approx. 25' × 45' = 1,125 sq. ft.) remains the single most sought-after and liquid property size across Punjab, including Chichawatni.

In this comprehensive guide, Siddhu Property Advisor breaks down the realistic 2026 construction costs for an A-Grade double-storey 5 Marla house with an estimated covered area of 1,950 to 2,100 sq. ft.

1. 5 Marla Covered Area Breakdown

A standard double-storey 5 Marla plan typically includes:

  • Ground Floor (Approx. 1,050 sq. ft.): Car Porch, Drawing Room, TV Lounge, 2 Bedrooms with Attached Baths, Kitchen, and Powder Room / Laundry.

  • First Floor (Approx. 950 sq. ft.): 2 Bedrooms with Attached Baths, Open Living Area / Lounge, Kitchenette / Balcony, and Terrace.

  • Rooftop / Mumty (Approx. 100 sq. ft.): Staircase Mumty & Water Tank Structure.

  • Total Covered Area: ~2,000 – 2,100 sq. ft.

3. Phase 2: Finishing Cost (A-Grade Luxury Standards)

Finishing turns a bare structure into a comfortable luxury home. The budget varies depending on personal taste, imported vs. local porcelain tiles, and woodwork.

  1. Floor & Wall Tiles:
- Porcelain double-glazed floor tiles (Master, Stile, Granito): Rs. 750,000 – 950,000
  1. Bathrooms & Sanitary Fittings:
- Vanities, commodes, mixer taps, shower sets (Porta, Master, Sonex): Rs. 450,000 – 600,000
  1. Woodwork & Cabinetry:
- Semi-solid ash-wood/deodar main doors, UV sheet wardrobes & acrylic kitchen cabinets: Rs. 850,000 – 1,150,000
  1. Windows & Glass:
- 1.6mm powder-coated aluminum or UPVC frames with 5mm tinted tempered glass: Rs. 320,000 – 420,000
  1. Electrical Fixtures & Wiring:
- 99.9% pure copper wiring (Pakistan Cables / Fast), LED concealed lights, designer chandeliers, fans (GFC / Pak / Royal): Rs. 550,000 – 700,000
  1. Paint & False Ceiling:
- Gypsum false ceiling design + Berger / Dulux velvet interior and rock-wall exterior finish: Rs. 400,000 – 550,000
  1. Main Gate & Metal Work:
- Designer laser-cut 14-gauge main gate and stainless steel stair railings: Rs. 250,000 – 350,000
Total Finishing Cost: Rs. 45 Lakh – Rs. 55 Lakh PKR (Approx. Rs. 2,200 – 2,700 / sq. ft.)

4. Total Budget Summary (2026)

  • Gray Structure: Rs. 52,00,000 – 60,00,000
  • Finishing (A-Grade): Rs. 45,00,000 – 55,00,000
Total Construction Budget: Rs. 97 Lakh – Rs. 1.15 Crore PKR (Excluding plot cost)*

5. Local Chichawatni Advice from Naeem Siddhu

If you are planning to build in Hayat Abad, Civil Lines, Housing Colony, or Model Town Chichawatni:

  1. Soil & Foundation Testing: Because parts of Tehsil Chichawatni are close to the agricultural canal network, always invest in proper compaction and solid damp-proof courses (DPC) with anti-termite spray.

  2. Approved Building Plan: Get your structural map cleared through the local Municipal Committee / TMA to ensure hassle-free utility connections (WAPDA electricity and Sui Gas).

  3. Verified Purchase First: Never start construction without verifying the computerized Fard Malkiat and physical possession boundary markers through our office.
