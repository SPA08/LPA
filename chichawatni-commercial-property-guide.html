Commercial Real Estate Insights

Chichawatni Commercial Property & Investment Guide

Shops, commercial plazas, main road frontages, rental yields, and high-ROI business property investments across Tower Road, Railway Road, GT Road Bypass, and Ghalla Mandi.

Tower Road Financial Hub

High footfall commercial avenue housing top commercial banks, corporate offices, mobile markets, and luxury retail shops. Home to Siddhu Property Advisor office.

Ghalla Mandi Commodity Exchange

One of Punjab's busiest agricultural trading markets. Prime commercial shops and godowns with steady commercial lease income from grain and cotton merchants.

GT Road Bypass Belt

Ideal for fuel stations, marriage halls, hotel plazas, warehouses, automobile showrooms, and large-scale commercial plots along National Highway N-5.

Railway Road & Circular Market

Traditional retail shopping hub for gold jewelry, fashion, footwear, electronics, and general consumer goods with high daily customer density.
