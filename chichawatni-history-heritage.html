History & Heritage of Chichawatni

The origin, 1913 artificial forest plantation, British canal colony urban planning, and historic evolution of Tehsil Chichawatni in Sahiwal District.

Part 1: Origins & Name

The Origins & Name of Chichawatni (وجود اور وجہ تسمیہ)

Chichawatni derives its historical name from the ancient Punjabi Chhachhar tribe and their traditional settlement village known as Watni (وٹنی). Before the British canal colony era, the region was an expanse of native scrubland along the Ravi river basin.

With the arrival of North Western Railway line in the late 19th century, Chichawatni emerged as a crucial railway station on the Karachi–Lahore–Peshawar ML-1 rail corridor, laying the foundation for modern urban expansion.

Part 2: The 1913 Forest

The Historic Chichawatni Forest (چیچہ وطنی کا تاریخی جنگل)

Established in 1913 by British forest conservationists, the Chichawatni Forest Reserve is the second-largest irrigated artificial forest in Pakistan (after Changa Manga), spanning over 9,000 acres (3,600+ hectares) along Grand Trunk Road (N-5).

Steam Locomotive Fuel Purpose

The forest was originally planted to produce firewood and timber for fueling North Western Railway steam locomotives and local agriculture.

Ecological Green Lung

Home to native Shisham (Dalbergia sissoo), Kikar (Acacia nilotica), Eucalyptus, wild deer, game birds, and natural biodiversity.

Irrigated by LBDC Canal System

Fed directly by dedicated irrigation channels from the Lower Bari Doab Canal (LBDC) network.

Part 3: Canal Colony Planning

British Canal Colony Urban Layout & 12 Bazaars

Between 1912 and 1916, British urban planners laid out Chichawatni as a master-planned Canal Colony Town. The town center was built in a radial grid surrounding Circular Road with 12 numbered bazaars (Bazar No. 1 to 12) radiating outwards.

The surrounding agricultural belt was divided into numbered revenue Chaks (such as Chak 40/12-L, Chak 39/12-L, and Chak 42/12-L), establishing a systematic land revenue framework that continues to form the legal basis of property ownership in Tehsil Chichawatni today.

Part 4: Modern Legacy

Modern Evolution & Property Legacy

From its historic 1913 forest origins to its status today as a major Tehsil of Sahiwal District, Chichawatni has transformed into a thriving residential and commercial center. Prime residential schemes like Hayat Abad, Model Town, Housing Scheme No. 1 & 2, and Civil Lines offer serene family living.
