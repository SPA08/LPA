Chichawatni PLRA Land Records & Legal Transfer Guide

Everything you need to know about Arazi Record Center (ARC) Chichawatni, computerized Fard Malkiat, Khewat & Khatooni verification, Sub-Registrar Registry, and FBR tax status.

Fard Malkiat (فرد ملکیت)

The official computerized title deed issued by Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA) verifying exact land ownership, share size, and mortgaged status.

Biometric Intiqal (انتقال)

Biometric mutation process at Arazi Record Center Chichawatni where buyer and seller verify thumb impressions on the official Punjab land database.

Khewat & Khasra (کھیوٹ، خسرہ)

Official revenue survey block numbers specifying exact land location within Chak 40/12-L, Chak 39/12-L, or parent revenue estate.

E-Stamp & Sale Deed

Judicial stamp paper generated online via Punjab e-Stamping portal, executed at Sub-Registrar Tehsil Chichawatni office.

5-Step Legal Property Transfer Procedure

1

Fard Baraye Bae (فرد برائے بیع) Issuance

Seller visits Arazi Record Center Chichawatni to obtain computerized Fard for sale purposes. The revenue officer locks the record to prevent dual sales.

2

Punjab e-Stamp Generation

Calculated via official FBR and DC valuation tables. Generated from National Bank or Bank of Punjab Chichawatni branches.

3

Sale Deed Draft (Baye Nama)

Prepared with exact property boundaries, square footage, agreed price, and witnesses by licensed document writers.

4

Sub-Registrar Execution

Buyer and seller appear before the Sub-Registrar Chichawatni with official CNICs and photos for legal deed endorsement.

5

Biometric Intiqal (Mutation)

Deed is submitted back to PLRA Arazi Record Center for computerized biometric mutation, transferring title permanently in government records.
