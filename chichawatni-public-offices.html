Government & Revenue Directory

Public & Revenue Offices in Chichawatni

Official directory of local government institutions, land record authorities, municipal offices, and utility providers operating in Tehsil Chichawatni.

Official Local Government Entity

Municipal Committee (MC Chichawatni)

The official local government body responsible for municipal administration, building plan sanctions, commercial mapping approval, sanitation, and street maintenance.

  • Functions: Building Maps, Commercial Permits, Sanitation
  • Jurisdiction: Urban Chichawatni & Notified Colonies
  • Location: MC Complex, Municipal Office Road
Computerised Land Record Authority

Arazi Record Center (PLRA)

Punjab Land Records Authority computerised center for issuing official Fard (ownership certificate), biometric property transfers, and record verification.

  • Services: Fard Malkiyat, Mutation (Intiqal), Biometrics
  • Jurisdiction: All Rural & Suburban Chaks (39/12-L, 40/12-L)
  • Location: Tehsil Revenue Complex, Chichawatni
Tehsil Revenue Administration

Assistant Commissioner & Sub-Registrar

Office of the Assistant Commissioner (AC) and Sub-Registrar handling registered sale deeds (Registry), stamp duty collection, and revenue appeals.

  • Services: Property Registry, Stamp Duty, Administrative Orders
  • Location: AC Courts & Courts Complex
  • Access: Civil Lines Road Corridor
Public Utility Offices

WAPDA (MEPCO) & Sui Gas (SNGPL)

Sub-divisional offices for electricity meter connections, Sui gas demand notices, title transfer on utility bills, and NOC verifications.

  • Services: Electricity/Gas Connections, Meter Name Transfer
  • Location: GT Road & Power House Road
  • Coverage: Entire Chichawatni Tehsil
