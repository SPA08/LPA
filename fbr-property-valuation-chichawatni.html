Official Reference Guide

FBR Property Valuation Chichawatni

Locality-specific official Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) valuation reference rates for stamp duty, withholding tax, and property registration across revenue chaks and colonies in Tehsil Chichawatni.

Official Tax Reference Disclaimer

Important Notice: These figures are official FBR valuation references used strictly for tax calculation purposes (withholding tax, stamp duty, gain tax) and may differ significantly from actual market transaction prices. Real estate market values in Chichawatni depend on street width, corner status, and negotiations.

FBR Valuation Rates by Revenue Chak

Chak 40/12-L Valuation Area

Includes Hayat Abad, Housing Colony, Ghaffor Town, and Gulberg Town.

  • Residential (Per Marla):PKR 140,000 - 220,000
  • Commercial (Per Marla):PKR 450,000 - 750,000
  • PLRA Authority:Arazi Record Center Chichawatni

Chak 39/12-L Valuation Area

Includes Garden Town, Defence Block, Canal Fort Colony, and Al Rehman Town.

  • Residential (Per Marla):PKR 125,000 - 190,000
  • Commercial (Per Marla):PKR 400,000 - 650,000
  • PLRA Authority:Tehsil Revenue Complex

GT Road Commercial Corridor

Main Highway frontage from Railway Crossing to Chichawatni Bypass.

  • Commercial Frontage:PKR 850,000 - 1,500,000 / Marla
  • Semi-Commercial:PKR 550,000 - 850,000 / Marla
  • FBR Status:Category I Prime Commercial
