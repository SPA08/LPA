Essential Real Estate FAQs

Key information on buying, record checks, and property advisory in Chichawatni.

Always verify the ownership record (Fard Malkiat) at the Arazi Record Center, ensure legal NOC status of the housing area, and inspect construction quality, utility connections (gas & electricity), and road access.
Ownership records are checked through the Arazi Record Center (ARC) Chichawatni (Punjab Land Records Authority) using computerized Fard copies and Aks-Shajra maps to verify khewat and khasra numbers.
Popular residential sectors include Hayat Abad (Tower Road / Chak 40/12-L), Model Town Phase I (DPDC approved), Housing Colony, and Green Town (Chak 39/12-L), offering 5, 7, 10, and 12 Marla residences.
