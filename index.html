FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Essential Real Estate FAQs

Clear, honest answers regarding residential purchases, PLRA record verification, and selling procedures in Tehsil Chichawatni.

1. What should I check before buying a house in Chichawatni?

Always verify the computerized ownership record (Fard Malkiat) at the Arazi Record Center (ARC) Chichawatni, confirm that the housing scheme or colony is legitimately recognized, inspect the damp-proof course (DPC) and construction quality, and check active Sui Gas and WAPDA electricity meter installations.

2. How do you verify property records (Fard Malkiat & Inteqal)?

Ownership records are verified through the Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA) computer system in Chichawatni. We verify the Khewat, Khatoni, and Khasra numbers against the official computerized record, and obtain an official Aks-Shajra map to confirm physical on-ground boundaries.

3. Which residential areas are best for family living?

Key residential locations in Chichawatni include Hayat Abad (Tower Road side), Model Town Phase I (DPDC approved), Housing Colony (planned government scheme with parks), and Civil Lines (peaceful administrative quarter with solid vintage houses).

4. Can SIDDHU Property Advisor help me sell my house or plot?

Yes. Through our Verified Seller Portal, you can submit your property details, record verified GPS coordinates, and add authentic on-site photos. Naeem Siddhu personally conducts a human review, evaluates current fair market value, and matches you with pre-qualified buyers.

5. How can I arrange an in-person site visit with Naeem Siddhu?
