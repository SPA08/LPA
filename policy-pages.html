Official Policies & Guidelines

Transparent terms, client privacy protection, and regulatory guidelines for real estate advisory in Chichawatni.

1. Privacy Policy

We value your privacy. Any personal information you provide to Siddhu Property Advisor, including contact details and property requirements, is kept strictly confidential and used solely for the purpose of assisting you with real estate transactions in Chichawatni. We never sell or share private client information with third-party telemarketers.

2. Terms & Conditions

By engaging with Siddhu Property Advisor, you agree to our terms of service. We provide advisory and brokerage services, connecting buyers with sellers. We act as intermediaries and advisors; however, final legal obligations rest with the contracting parties involved in the transaction. All agreements must be executed on official Punjab judicial stamp paper.

3. Disclaimer

All property information is provided based on data from owners and physical site verifications. While we strive for rigorous accuracy, SIDDHU Property Advisor makes no absolute warranties regarding changing municipal boundaries or unannounced fiscal policy shifts. Independent buyer verification prior to monetary exchange is always conducted.

4. Property Verification Policy

We strongly advise and assist all buyers to independently verify property ownership (Fard Malkiat) through the Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA) Arazi Record Center Chichawatni or the respective housing society office prior to finalizing any financial transaction.

5. Listing Information Policy

Property prices and availability in Chichawatni are subject to owner negotiations and market dynamics. Listings on our website or social media channels represent verified current inventory at publication and do not constitute an irrevocable binding offer.

6. Information Verification Policy

We conduct physical site inspections of properties we list to check structure, utilities, and road width. Buyers receive accompanied access to verify exact boundary lines (Nishan-Dahi) and utility accounts (MEPCO / SNGPL) before deed signature.

7. AML / KYC Compliance Notice

In compliance with national regulatory guidelines, we require valid National Identity Cards (CNIC) from both buyers and sellers to prevent fraud and ensure transparent records. We do not claim government certifications unless explicitly documented.

8. Cookie Policy

Our website uses only essential browser storage and lightweight session cookies to maintain filter preferences and ensure rapid page loading. No invasive advertising tracking cookies are deployed.
