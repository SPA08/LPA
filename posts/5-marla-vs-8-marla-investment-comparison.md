## 2. Living Comfort & Family Dynamics

### Why Choose 5 Marla?
* **Compact & Efficient:** Ideal for families of 3 to 6 members who prefer low-maintenance living without excess unused space.
* **Lower Utility Overheads:** Heating and cooling costs are substantially lower, and rooftop solar installations (5 kW system) are sufficient to run the entire house off-grid.
* **Accessible Entry Budget:** Allows young professionals and overseas buyers with limited capital to own a standalone freehold residential home.

### Why Choose 8 Marla?
* **Dual Family / Guest Privacy:** Allows a completely isolated formal drawing/dining room and a spacious family lounge without compromising on bedroom dimensions.
* **Dual Car Parking:** An essential requirement for multi-car households who do not want to park vehicles on the street.
* **Dedicated Servant Quarters:** Ample rooftop space for domestic staff quarters and separate storage rooms.

## 3. Investment & Resale Perspective in Chichawatni

### Resale Speed (Liquidity):
In Chichawatni's local market (including Hayat Abad, Housing Scheme, Model Town, and Civil Lines), **5 Marla houses sell faster** because the ticket price fits the purchasing power of local businessmen, government officers, and remittance-backed families.
