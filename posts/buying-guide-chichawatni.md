# A Complete Guide to Buying Property in Chichawatni

Purchasing a property in Chichawatni requires a solid understanding of local market dynamics, residential colonies, and the Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA) verification process.

## 1. Choose the Right Location
Chichawatni offers a variety of residential sectors. If you are looking for modern amenities, **Subhani Garden** or **Rehman Town** might be ideal. For those preferring proximity to the main bazaar and central hubs, **Housing Colony** is a prime choice.

## 2. Verify the Fard (Property Record)
Never purchase a property without verifying the **Fard-e-Malkiat** (Record of Rights). 
At Siddhu Property Advisor, we ensure every transaction is backed by clear, verified land records to protect your investment.

## 3. Understand Market Valuation
Property prices vary significantly based on:
* Proximity to major roads (like GT Road)
* Corner vs. non-corner plots
* Commercial vs. residential zoning
