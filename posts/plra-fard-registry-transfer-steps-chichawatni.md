# Step-by-Step PLRA Fard & Registry Process in Tehsil Chichawatni

Purchasing real estate in Punjab without verifying land records can lead to legal disputes, boundary conflicts, or financial loss. With the modernization of the **Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA)** and the computerized **Arazi Record Center (ARC) Chichawatni**, land records are more transparent than ever before.

In this guide, **Naeem Siddhu** outlines the exact 5-step official procedure to safely verify, purchase, and transfer property in Tehsil Chichawatni.

---

## 1. Step 1: Obtaining Computerized Fard Malkiat (فرد برائے بیع)

Before paying any token money or advance:
1. **Visit Arazi Record Center Chichawatni:** Located near the Judicial Complex / Bypass Road.
2. **Required Documents:** CNIC of the owner and exact Khewat / Khatooni number or Chak name (e.g., Chak 40/12-L, Chak 39/12-L).
3. **Verify Ownership & Encumbrance:** Ensure the Fard does not show any bank mortgage (*Rahn*), court stay orders, or unauthorized co-shares.
4. **Fard Baraye Bai (فرد برائے بیع):** Obtain the official copy issued specifically for sale purpose.

---

## 2. Step 2: On-Ground Physical Demarcation (*Nishandehi*)

A paper document is only valid if physical possession corresponds to the revenue map:
* Always verify the boundary corners with local revenue officials (*Patwari / Qanungo*) and physical neighbors.
* Check road width, access rights, and municipal drainage connectivity.

---

## 3. Step 3: Generating E-Stamp Papers & Tax Challans

Under Punjab Government regulations, traditional manual stamp papers have been replaced with **Punjab E-Stamping**:
* **DC Valuation Rate:** The official collector DC rate for the specific Chak/Colony is calculated via the e-Stamping portal.
* **Stamp Duty & TMA Tax:** Calculated based on the registered value of the transaction.
* **FBR Tax Status (Section 236K & 236C):** 
  - Filer vs. Late Filer vs. Non-Filer tax rates apply for both Buyer Advance Tax (236K) and Seller Gain Tax (236C). Being an active tax filer saves substantial transfer costs.

---

## 4. Step 4: Execution at Sub-Registrar Office Chichawatni

1. The sale deed (*Bayan-e-Halfi / Registry*) is typed and signed by buyer and seller in the presence of two reputable local witnesses.
2. Both parties appear before the **Sub-Registrar Tehsil Chichawatni**.
3. Photographs and biometric thumb impressions are recorded officially in the Sub-Registrar registry book.

---

## 5. Step 5: Biometric Intiqal (انتقال) & Mutation Update

For rural and computerized revenue estates:
* The transaction is forwarded to the Arazi Record Center database where final biometric mutation (*Intiqal*) is entered.
* Once the Intiqal is approved by the Revenue Officer (*Tehsildar / Naib Tehsildar*), the buyer's name is officially updated on the Punjab digital land portal (`punjab-zameen.gov.pk`).
