## 1. Get Your Documents Verified in Advance

The number one reason deals collapse during negotiations is missing or ambiguous documentation. Before listing your property:
* Obtain a fresh computerized **Fard Malkiat** showing clear title.
* Gather original Registry, previous mutation documents, and approved building plans.
* Collect recent paid bills for electricity (MEPCO/WAPDA), Sui Gas, and municipal taxes.

When prospective buyers see complete legal clarity upfront, decision-making becomes swift.

## 3. High-Impact Staging & Minor Touch-ups

First impressions make all the difference during a site visit:
* **Fresh Paint & Touch-ups:** A fresh coat of neutral paint on the main entrance and front elevation dramatically enhances curb appeal.
* **Fix Minor Plumbing & Electrical:** Repair leaking taps, loose door handles, and ensure all lights in the car porch and drawing room are working.
* **Deep Clean & Declutter:** Clean tiled floors and keep the car porch clear of clutter.
